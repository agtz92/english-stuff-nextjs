  Looking for underrated indie games that fit a busy schedule? Here are five
  lesser-known picks you can finish during a lunch break, in a single evening,
  or over a relaxed weekend. Each one comes with a one-line hook, a play length,
  and platform notes, so you can decide without digging through a dozen store
  pages.


  One quick caveat: prices and platform lists change over time, so confirm them
  on the store page before you buy.


  ## Underrated indie games: how we define a quick pick


  We sort these games by time budget rather than genre, because time is the
  constraint most readers hit first. Our bands are simple:


  - **Under 1 hour:** a single short session

  - **One evening:** roughly 1 to 3 hours

  - **Flexible:** a game you can stretch or shorten depending on how much you
  explore


  The industry's own definitions help here. [itch.io's short
  tag](https://itch.io/games/new-and-popular/tag-best/tag-short) describes games
  with short playtimes as ones that can usually be completed in one sitting,
  which is the standard we use for the first band. Lengths below are shown as
  ranges because skill level and completion goals change how long any game
  takes.


  Smaller projects are also becoming a bigger part of the conversation. GDC's
  2025 State of the Game Industry report, which surveyed over 3,000 developers,
  found that half of all developers are self-funding their games. In our view,
  tight budgets often lead to tight design, and that is a big reason short games
  can feel so complete.

  ## Under an hour: two picks for a short break


  ### Inkslinger


  Four endings in less than an hour means one evening can show you several ways
  a story can land.


  Inkslinger is the shortest pick on this list. According to a [Steam curator
  list dedicated to underrated
  indies](https://store.steampowered.com/curator/36805762-Underrated-Indies/?snr=1_1050_curatorsreviewing_),
  it takes less than 1 hour to play through, yet it still has four different
  endings. That makes it a good fit for readers who like to explore outcomes and
  want a reason to start again without committing a whole weekend.


  - **Play length:** Less than 1 hour

  - **Platform:** PC (Steam)

  - **Best for:** A fast, replayable test of your curiosity


  ### EMMA The Story


  A 15-minute kinetic novel you can read on a lunch break.


  If you have ever wished for a story without a big time commitment, EMMA The
  Story is a strong option. A kinetic novel is a text-driven story with little
  or no player choice, so you mostly read and move forward. The same curator
  page lists it at $0.99 on Steam, with a description that frames it as a
  15-minute experience.


  - **Play length:** About 15 minutes

  - **Platform:** PC (Steam)

  - **Best for:** Readers who want a complete story in one sitting

  ## One evening: two games for a single night

  A sequel built for one focused night, with a clear finish line.


  A typical player can finish Figment 2 in 2 to 3 hours, according to a

  - **Play length:** 2 to 3 hours

  - **Platform:** Check the store listing for your device

  - **Best for:** A complete adventure without a multi-day commitment


  ### Moonleap


  A journey-first game with a predictable time budget.


  Moonleap sits in a similar range. The same source expects the journey to take
  2 to 4 hours, depending on player skill and how much of the game you complete.
  If you want to plan your evening, the longer end of the range is the safer bet
  for players who like to explore every corner.


  - **Play length:** 2 to 4 hours

  - **Platform:** Check the store listing for your device

  - **Best for:** Players who want a bit more than a single hour


  ## A flexible pick: A Short Hike


  A Short Hike is a gentle exploration game where your own pace largely sets how
  long you spend with it. It launched on Steam in July 2019 for $7.99, and Valve
  lists it as verified for Steam Deck, which makes it a good match for handheld
  play. It later arrived on Nintendo Switch in August 2020, followed by PS4 and
  Xbox One in November 2021, according to [SteamDB's entry for the

  - **Play length:** Flexible; depends on how much you explore

  - **Platforms:** Steam (Steam Deck verified), Nintendo Switch, PS4, Xbox One

  - **Best for:** Low-pressure play on the couch or a handheld

  ## How to choose in under a minute


  Use this quick checklist before you buy:


  1. Match the game to the time you actually have.

  2. Check the platform on the store page, since availability changes.

  3. Look for a clear length range, not just the word short.

  4. Read one recent player review for pacing and difficulty.

  5. Skip any game whose run time you cannot fit into your window.

  ## Frequently asked questions


  ### What makes an indie game underrated?


  An underrated indie game is well made and well liked by the players who find
  it, but it rarely appears on mainstream best-of lists. That is why we set
  aside household names and focus on titles that are easy to miss.


  ### How long is a short indie game?


  Under an hour counts as very short, while one to three hours is a common range
  for a single-evening game. Run times vary with skill and with how much of the
  game you chase, so treat any number as a guide.


  ### Are there free underrated indie games?


  Some indie games are free or pay-what-you-want. Availability changes often, so
  check the store's free section or a curated list, and read the details before
  you download.


  ### What is the difference between a kinetic novel and a visual novel?


  A kinetic novel is mostly text with little or no player choice. A visual novel
  usually includes branching choices that change how the story unfolds. Both are
  story-first, but visual novels typically give you more say in the outcome.


  ### Where can I check price history?


  SteamDB, linked above, tracks price history for many Steam games. Use it to
  see whether a current price is a good deal or just a temporary sale.


  ## Final takeaway


  Underrated indie games are easiest to enjoy when you choose by time rather
  than hype. Start with Inkslinger or EMMA The Story if you have an hour,
  Figment 2 or Moonleap for a full evening, and A Short Hike when you want
  something flexible. Confirm platforms and prices before you buy, and you will
  spend your time on a game you can actually finish.
