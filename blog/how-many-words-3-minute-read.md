  So, how many words is a 3 minute read? For most adults reading silently, it's
  about 700 words. A large 2019 review of 190 studies puts the average silent
  reading speed for English non-fiction at 238 words per minute, and three
  minutes at that pace comes to roughly 714 words. Depending on the reader and
  the material, the realistic range runs from about 525 to 900 words.

  ## Where These Numbers Come From


  The most cited source is [Brysbaert's 2019 meta-analysis in the Journal of
  Memory and
  Language](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0749596X19300786).
  It pulled together 190 studies of reading rate and found that most adults read
  English non-fiction silently at somewhere between 175 and 300 words per
  minute. The average was 238 for non-fiction and 260 for fiction. Oral reading
  averaged 183 words per minute, based on 77 studies and nearly 6,000
  participants.


  The review also found slower rates for children, older adults, and readers who
  use English as a second language. If your audience is young or international,
  expect some readers to take a little longer than the average.


  A separate study in [Psychologica
  Belgica](https://psychologicabelgica.com/articles/10.5334/pb.1189) also found
  that adults reading silently were well below 300 words per minute, which
  supports the same picture.


  ## Is 300 Words Per Minute the Average?


  No. It's a figure you'll see often online, but it sits at the top of the
  typical range rather than the middle. The British Psychological Society's
  Research Digest offers an [accessible summary of the same
  review](https://www.bps.org.uk/research-digest/most-comprehensive-review-date-finds-average-persons-reading-speed-slower),
  and its main point is that the average reader is slower than the popular
  number suggests.


  That matters for planning. At 300 words per minute, three minutes covers 900
  words, which is the upper edge of what most adults manage, not a typical
  result.

  ## Why Do Sources Give Such Different Answers?


  Search this question and you'll find numbers from about 300 to 600 words,
  sometimes higher. Three factors explain most of the gap.


  - **Reading mode.** Silent reading is faster than reading aloud. A script you
  read to an audience will always land lower than a page you read to yourself.

  - **Material.** Fiction is read slightly faster than non-fiction in the 2019
  review, and dense technical text slows every reader down.

  - **Reader.** Age, language background, and familiarity with the subject all
  shift the number.


  Lower figures often assume a slower pace, such as a presentation. Higher
  figures often reflect fast silent readers. Neither is wrong for its own use
  case, but for a typical article read silently, the research points to about
  700 words.


  Word counts can also shift from tool to tool. Counters apply different
  segmentation rules to hyphenated terms, numbers, and symbols, as [Wikipedia's
  entry on word count](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Word_count) explains. A
  tool that counts a hyphenated term as two words will report a higher total
  than one that counts it as one.


  ## Why We Aim for 400 to 700 Words


  Here is where the numbers meet our format. At 133 words per minute, 400 words
  takes three minutes. At 233 words per minute, 700 words does. In plain terms,
  400 words suits a relaxed pace and a quick skim, while 700 words sits close to
  the silent non-fiction average.


  We think that band is the sweet spot for news and explainers. This is an
  editorial choice rather than a rule from the research. Many readers on phones
  scan before they commit, and a piece in this range leaves room for a clear
  hook, a few subheads, and one practical takeaway without padding. It's the
  same logic behind the format at 3minread. Short-form storytelling works the
  same way in other formats too, as seen in [Microdramas Explained: Why
  One-Minute Soap Operas Are
  Everywhere](https://www.3minread.com/microdramas-explained-one-minute-soap-operas).


  Faster is not always better. The [Wikipedia overview of speed
  reading](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speed_reading) notes that comprehension
  tends to fall once reading speed climbs past roughly 400 to 500 words per
  minute. That's why we don't write for the top of the range just to look
  efficient.

  ## How Do You Calculate Reading Time for Any Length?


  You can reuse the same math for any length with three steps:


  1. Pick a speed. For silent non-fiction, use 238 words per minute. For reading
  aloud, use 183.

  2. To find words for a given time, multiply minutes by speed. Three minutes at
  238 is about 714 words.

  3. To find time for a text, divide the word count by speed. A 1,000-word guide
  at 238 words per minute takes about 4.2 minutes.


  Here are some quick conversions at the silent non-fiction average:


  - 1 minute: about 238 words

  - 3 minutes: about 714 words

  - 5 minutes: about 1,190 words

  - 10 minutes: about 2,380 words


  If you're writing for audio, such as a podcast script or voice-over, plan
  closer to 550 words for three minutes, since the script will be read aloud. If
  you're writing for silent reading on a screen, 700 words is a solid target.


  ## What Does This Mean for Web Articles?


  Reading speed is only half the story online. Readers arrive from search, skim
  headings, and often read on a phone. In our view, a 700-word article with
  clear subheads usually feels quicker than a dense 500-word block of text, so
  structure matters as much as the count.


  A few habits help:


  - Answer the question in the first two or three lines.

  - Keep paragraphs short and focused on one idea.

  - Add a subhead every 150 to 250 words.

  - Check the count with the same tool you'll publish with.

  ## Frequently Asked Questions


  ### How many words is a 3 minute speech?


  Speeches usually run slower than silent reading because of pauses, emphasis,
  and audience reactions. Since delivery varies so much, the most reliable
  method is to time a rehearsal with your own script and adjust from there.


  ### What is the average reading speed in words per minute?


  For silent reading of English non-fiction, the average adult manages about 238
  words per minute, according to Brysbaert's 2019 review. Fiction averages about
  260, and reading aloud averages about 183.


  ### How long does it take to read 1,000 words?


  At the non-fiction average of 238 words per minute, about four minutes. Slower
  readers, or material that is dense or technical, will take longer.


  ### Is 300 words per minute the average reading speed?


  No. It's a popular figure near the top of the typical range. The average in
  the 2019 review was lower, at 238 words per minute for non-fiction.


  ### Why does my word counter give a different number?


  Counters use different rules for hyphenated words, numbers, and symbols. Pick
  one tool and use it consistently across your drafts.


  ## The Bottom Line


  A 3-minute read is about 700 words for most silent readers of non-fiction,
  with a realistic range from roughly 525 to 900. Start with 238 words per
  minute as your baseline, multiply for any length you need, and adjust for
  reading aloud or unusual material. That simple math is also why 3minread keeps
