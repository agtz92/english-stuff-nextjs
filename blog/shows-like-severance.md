  ## What Makes Severance Work


  Severance follows employees at Lumon Industries who undergo a procedure that
  splits their memories. Their 'innie' selves know only the office, while their
  'outie' selves live ordinary lives with no idea what happens downstairs. The
  series debuted on Apple TV+ in February 2022 (Wikipedia)).


  The awards run was just as big. Severance led all nominees at the 2025
  Primetime Emmy Awards with 27 nominations, and it finished with eight Emmys
  overall, including Creative Arts wins, according to
  [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/timlammers/2025/09/14/how-many-emmy-awards-did-severance-win-at-the-2025-emmys/).
  Britt Lower won Best Actress in a Drama Series for Season 2
  ([IndieWire](https://www.indiewire.com/awards/results/britt-lower-wins-emmy-best-actress-drama-severance-1235150581/)),
  and Tramell Tillman became the first Black actor to win an Emmy for
  Outstanding Supporting Actor in a Drama Series
  ([Time](https://time.com/7316893/tramell-tillman-wins-emmy-severance/)).


  So what makes a show feel like Severance? We used three checks for every pick:


  - **Someone controls what you know.** A company, institution, or group decides
  what the characters are allowed to learn.

  - **Identity or memory splits or disappears.** The characters are not quite
  who they were before.

  - **The reveals reward attention.** Each episode reframes what came before it.

  ## The 7 Shows Like Severance


  ### 1. Silo


  Silo is the closest match for viewers who love the feeling that the company
  knows more than you do. It is set in a vast underground silo where residents
  are told the outside world is off limits. Rebecca Ferguson plays Juliette, who
  starts asking questions the community's rules forbid, and every answer opens a
  new door. Like Lumon, the setup runs on controlled information.


  **Where to watch:** Silo has been listed on Apple TV, the same service as
  Severance. Listings shift, so confirm on JustWatch before you press play.


  ### 2. Homecoming


  Julia Roberts plays Heidi Bergman, a caseworker at a transitional program who
  has no memory of her earlier job. That gap is the closest parallel to
  Severance's memory split: a workplace quietly shaping what its people
  remember. If you liked the uneasy, slow pace of Mark's early episodes, this
  one will feel familiar.


  **Where to watch:** Check JustWatch for the current listing.


  ### 3. Upload


  Upload is a comedy, but its premise is pure corporate control. After death,
  people are moved into a digital afterlife run by a company, and their paid
  tiers decide how comfortable that afterlife is. It is a lighter pick when
  Severance feels too heavy, and it still asks who gets to own your experience.


  **Where to watch:** Amazon Prime Video.


  ### 4. Black Mirror


  Black Mirror is the anthology pick. Each episode is a standalone story about
  technology bending human behavior. 'Nosedive' is the best example of a system
  that scores people in real time, a theme that echoes Severance's unease about
  being measured and managed.


  **Where to watch:** Netflix.


  ### 5. Dark


  Dark is the puzzle box. This German series layers time travel over a small
  town's buried family secrets, and almost nothing is what it seems on first
  viewing. If you enjoy Severance's reveals that recontextualize earlier scenes,
  Dark rewards a rewatch.


  **Where to watch:** Check JustWatch for the current listing.


  ### 6. Devs


  Devs is the closest to a tech-company thriller. Alex Garland's limited series
  follows engineer Lily Chan, who investigates her partner's death at a
  secretive tech company where a hidden project seems to see the future. Its
  clean, controlled visuals and campus setting echo the clinical hallways of
  Lumon.


  **Where to watch:** Check JustWatch for the current listing. At eight
  episodes, it works as a single-weekend binge.


  ### 7. Fringe


  Fringe is for fans of split selves and institutional secrets. J.J. Abrams'
  series follows FBI agent Olivia Dunham and the unorthodox Walter Bishop as
  they investigate strange cases tied to a secretive science program. Its
  alternate versions of people echo Severance's innie and outie.


  **Where to watch:** Check JustWatch for the current listing.

  ## What to Watch While You Wait for Season 3


  If you want the closest tone, start with Homecoming or Devs. Both feel
  controlled and quiet, like Lumon's floors. For a bigger hook, go with Silo or
  Dark. At 3minread, we'd pace it at one episode a night, so the mystery lasts
  as long as the wait.


  ## Frequently Asked Questions


  ### Is there a Season 3 of Severance, and when does it come out?


  Apple renewed Severance for a third season in March 2025, but it has not
  announced a premiere date, episode count, or trailer. Any date you see online
  is an estimate.


  ### What is Severance about?


  Severance follows workers at Lumon Industries whose memories are split between
  work and home. Their work selves, the innies, have no knowledge of their
  outside lives, and the outies have no knowledge of the office.


  ### Is Silo like Severance?


  Yes, in tone. Both shows put characters inside controlled systems and reveal
  the truth slowly, though Silo leans more into an underground conspiracy.


  ### Is Black Mirror like Severance?


  Partly. Black Mirror shares Severance's unease about technology and corporate
  control, but each episode is a standalone story rather than one continuing
  mystery.


  ### Where can I stream Severance?


  Severance streams on Apple TV. Listings can change, so confirm the current
  platform on JustWatch before you start.


  ## Final Takeaway


  If you want more of Severance's workplace unease, start with Silo for
  institutional secrets, Homecoming for memory gaps, or Devs for the tech-campus
  chill. Each pick works on its own, and together they cover everything that
  made the show so hard to stop watching.
