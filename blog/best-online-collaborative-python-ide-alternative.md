  The article says Colab provides a Jupyter Notebook environment with 12 GB of
  RAM and an Intel Xeon processor. That's enough for quick experiments and
  prototyping, but it's not a substitute for a powerful desktop setup on heavy

  ### Can I use Google Colab for real-time collaboration?


  Colab is presented as a collaborative online Python IDE, and the article notes
  that online IDEs let users work on projects together in real time. Because it
  runs in the browser, you can also use it from any computer with an internet
