  If you have already put dozens of hours into Stardew Valley and still want
  that calm, seasonal rhythm, you are in good company. The best games like
  Stardew Valley share three things: a relaxed daily loop, freedom to set your
  own pace, and small wins that add up over time. Below are six farming and life
  sims worth your next weekend, each compared on pacing, depth, and co-op so you
  can choose quickly.


  ## How we compare pace, depth, and co-op


  These ratings are our editorial judgment, based on the criteria below rather
  than a formal review score:


  - **Pace:** How hard the game pushes you. Low means no hard deadlines; high
  means timed tasks, energy limits, or story beats that demand attention.

  - **Depth:** How many systems sit beside farming, such as combat, crafting
  trees, romance, exploration, or magic.

  - **Co-op:** Whether you can play with friends, and whether that needs a
  subscription or a specific platform.


  ## 1. Story of Seasons: Friends of Mineral Town


  This is the closest thing to a classic town sim. The calendar moves in
  seasons, you grow crops, raise animals, and get to know townspeople one
  conversation at a time.


  ### Pacing and depth


  Pace is low to moderate. Days have a clear loop of planting, feeding animals,
  and visiting shops, but nothing feels rushed. Depth is moderate: relationships
  and festivals add structure, while the farming systems stay simple and
  readable.


  ### Co-op


  This is primarily a single-player experience, so treat it as a quiet solo
  pick. If you want to share the farm, look at the options below.

  ## 2. Coral Island


  Coral Island feels modern and bright. You restore a tropical island, fish,
  mine, and build relationships with a larger cast than most cozy sims offer.


  ### Pacing and depth


  Pace is low to moderate, with more variety in daily tasks than Stardew. Depth
  is high because mining, fishing, and island projects all connect. If you like
  tracking several systems at once, this is a strong step up.


  ### Co-op


  Online co-op with friends is the main draw. It suits couples or friend groups
  who want to share chores without losing their own progress.


  ## 3. Sun Haven


  If you like Stardew's farm but want a stronger RPG backbone, Sun Haven is the
  pick. It mixes farming with quests, combat, and skill progression.


  ### Pacing and depth


  Pace is moderate. Combat and dungeon runs can speed things up, but you can
  still spend an entire afternoon planting and fishing. Depth is high, with
  skills and gear that give your choices more weight.


  ### Co-op


  Online co-op lets you tackle dungeons together, which is the clearest reason
  to choose it over a slower farm sim.


  ## 4. Fae Farm


  Fae Farm trades some of Stardew's simplicity for exploration and a light
  fantasy story. The world opens up through magic and fae-related quests.


  ### Pacing and depth


  Pace is low, so you can explore at your own speed. Depth is medium to high:
  magic, exploration, and relationships give you several reasons to log in. The
  tone is gentler than most RPGs.


  ### Co-op


  Co-op is available, but check the store page for your platform before you buy,
  since multiplayer details can vary by version.
mk3: >-
  ## 5. Animal Crossing: New Horizons


  Animal Crossing is less about a farm and more about a life on an island. You
  decorate, design paths, collect, and visit friends at your own pace.


  ### Pacing and depth


  Pace is very low. There are no hard deadlines, and the real-time calendar
  means you play in short sessions. Depth is low to medium: crafting and design
  are rich, but there is little combat and no deep skill tree.


  ### Co-op


  Up to eight players can share an island. Online play requires a Nintendo
  Switch Online subscription, so factor that cost in if you want to visit
  friends remotely.


  ## 6. Fields of Mistria


  Fields of Mistria is the newest pick on this list. Early access began in
  August 2024, and the full release arrived in August 2026, according to [Fields
  of Mistria on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fields_of_Mistria).


  ### Pacing and depth


  Pace is moderate. Farming tasks sit alongside combat and story, so your day
  can shift quickly. Depth is high, with magic, romance, and mining-style
  progression.


  ### Co-op


  Multiplayer is available. Confirm the current options on the store page,
  because full-release features may differ from early access.

  ## Who these games are not for


  Not every pick suits every player. Animal Crossing offers little challenge if
  you want a story-driven RPG. Sun Haven's combat may feel like a chore if you
  only came for the farm. Fields of Mistria is a newer release, so expect
  ongoing updates and balance changes after launch. If you are still deciding
  how much challenge you want, our [Best Cozy Games for
  Beginners](https://www.3minread.com/best-cozy-games-beginners) guide covers
  the gentlest starting points.


  ## Co-op on PC and consoles


  Stardew Valley is the benchmark for co-op in this genre. According to the
  [Stardew Valley Wiki's multiplayer
  page](https://wiki.stardewvalley.net/Multiplayer), a fan-run resource, version
  1.6 raised the PC player cap to eight. Console caps are lower and differ by
  version, so check the platform details before you invite friends.


  Stardew also has no crossplay, so if your friends are on a different platform,
  you will need to find a title that supports it, confirmed on its store page.


  ## Why Stardew's pricing matters when you choose


  Stardew Valley has sold over 50 million units as of February 2026, according
  to ConcernedApe, as [reported by

  That matters when you compare alternatives. Check whether a game has paid

  cheaper game with a large paid DLC may cost more in the end.

  ## Questions readers ask


  ### What game is most similar to Stardew Valley?


  Story of Seasons: Friends of Mineral Town is the closest in tone and
  structure. If you want more systems, Coral Island and Sun Haven add co-op and
  deeper progression.


  ### Are there multiplayer games like Stardew Valley?


  Yes. Coral Island and Sun Haven both offer online co-op, and Animal Crossing:
  New Horizons supports up to eight players on one island with a Nintendo Switch
  Online subscription.


  ### Can you play Stardew Valley co-op across platforms?


  No. Stardew Valley does not support crossplay, so friends must be on the same
  platform to play together.


  ### Do Stardew Valley updates cost extra?


  No. Major content updates have been free, which is one reason the game has
  such a loyal following. Other games in this genre may use paid expansions, so
  check before you buy.


  ## The takeaway


  For a slow, traditional farm, pick Story of Seasons. For co-op with a friend,
  choose Coral Island or Sun Haven. If you want a social island with very low
  pressure, Animal Crossing is the easiest recommendation. Fae Farm and Fields
  of Mistria suit players who want more story and magic. Whatever you choose,
  confirm the co-op details on the store page for your platform.
